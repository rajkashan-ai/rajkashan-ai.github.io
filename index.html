About

A great product and good distribution are both essential to a successful business. What motivates me is building products customers love, and turning them into commercial success.

I've worked in product since 2013, owning outcomes across growth, internationalisation, B2C, B2B and back office. Most of that time was at Trainline, where I went from senior product owner to product director for growth, then B2B, then several streams at once.

Before product, I spent over a decade on the technical side. I started as a web developer at Capgemini in 1999, moved into solution design, and joined Trainline as subject matter expert on a major re-platform.

Today I'm a Partner at High Intent Labs. I run live, hands-on courses that take product managers from AI user to AI builder in four weeks. They leave with working agents running in their own stack, with no engineering team needed.

Interests

Most of what I think about is where AI meets product work. I write about it most weeks.

Agents that move a number

An agent that writes the weekly report saves you forty minutes. One that finds the step where users give up can change activation. I'm interested in the second kind.

An agent is a product

It needs what any product needs: an outcome, scope, acceptance criteria, and a clear point where a person signs off.

Agents that don't waste

Agents re-read the same context constantly, and the cost is invisible. Good design caches what never changes and never looks up the same fact twice.

Who owns the mistake?

When an agent gets something wrong, is it the builder, the person who ran it, the vendor, or whoever signed off? I keep coming back to this one.

Groundwork first

Tools keep getting better at execution. Finding the right problem still means sitting with people and seeing where the friction is.
